About Us

Solanda Enterprises, Inc., founded on May 10, 1976, combines the names of two streets—Solana, meaning "sun," and Anda, meaning "to move"—reflecting the company's commitment to progress. Initially a regional distributor of spare parts and fishing accessories, Solanda quickly grew into the largest distributor of small gasoline engines, securing exclusive distributorships for marine engines, industrial gensets, power tools, construction equipment, and solar water heaters.

With eight specialized sales departments and a vast network of over 600 dealers across the country, Solanda is now a respected leader in construction, agricultural, fishing, and industrial machinery. The company remains dedicated to offering high-quality products and prioritizing customer satisfaction and after-sales service.
